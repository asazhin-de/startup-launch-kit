Every startup begins with a problem that will not go away. Ours was simple: by the time the plov reached the guests at the far end of the table, it was cold. Our first website had the same problem. It lived on one server, in one country, and visitors far away waited.

So we moved the kitchen closer to the guests. Our site runs on Cloudflare's network in more than 330 cities, so the page is served from a location near each visitor, whether they are in Tashkent, Almaty or Baku.

## No cold starts, no cold plov

Cloudflare Workers start instantly, so there is no "warming up" when the first visitor of the day arrives. We would like to say the same about our kazan, but the kazan still needs about an hour.
